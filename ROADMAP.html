Testing
Building-transfer denylist

Any completed empty structure transfers unless it is a Command Center, superweapon, science/advanced-tech building, Internet Center, Airfield, or fake structure. This avoids unreliable faction category flags and enables USA defenses/Drop Zones, GLA Black Markets, factories, and empty tunnels.

Done
Contextual Diplomacy layout

Money mode shows three equal-width transfer buttons. Selecting an eligible structure replaces them with one full-width TRANSFER BLDG button using a smaller adaptive font.

Planned
Ordinary garrisons

Repair the garrison capture state so a transferred bunker remains with its recipient after transferred occupants exit.

Planned
China Internet Center

Transfer contained Hackers and explicitly restart their cash-generation activity for the new owner.

Testing
Production buildings

Empty production buildings are enabled. Cross-faction units still obey the recipient's sciences, so units such as the Nuke Cannon can remain unavailable without the corresponding China General Science.

Deferred
Airfields and occupied tunnels

Docked aircraft require specialized ownership migration. Empty tunnels are enabled; a tunnel network containing units remains blocked.

Current design decision
Command Centers, Strategy/advanced-tech buildings, and superweapons remain non-transferable. Their powers, sciences, limits, timers, and defeat state are player-level; allies can already operate their useful controls through shared control.
