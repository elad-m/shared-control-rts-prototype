Decided
Done
Immediate financial support

Players can send $500, $1,000, or $5,000 from their own wallet to an active ally through Diplomacy.

Removed
No building ownership transfer

Shared control already lets allies operate buildings and construct an economic base for one another after sending funds. Ownership transfer duplicated that workflow while introducing power-grid, collector, science, garrison, airfield, tunnel, upgrade, and defeat-state edge cases.

Current design decision
Transfer funds, not ownership. The recipient can use the money directly, while either ally can use shared control to build and manage infrastructure belonging to the recipient.

Optional mixed allied transports and garrisons

A host-controlled Allow Mixed Allied Garrisons option now gates shared passenger vehicles, shared garrison space, and allied-pilot promotion. It is off by default and synchronized to every client. Owned structures keep their owner's flag; civilian garrison leadership passes only when the current leader has no occupants left. Complete the focused two-PC enabled/disabled checklist before marking this done.
